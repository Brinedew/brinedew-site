### 1. Material Defects Blocking `FL` (Alex Upheld Narrowly)

1. **Dimensional and conceptual error in Row 2 (`Aging` — demographic, line 74):**
   The table currently pairs `**Gompertz slope** ($b$, rate of rise of log-hazard in $\mu(t) = a e^{bt}$)` with `or model-free **age-specific rise in mortality rate** $\mu'(t)$`. That is mathematically wrong:
   - The Gompertz slope $b = \frac{d}{dt}\ln\mu(t)$ is a **relative** rate of hazard increase with dimensions of $\text{time}^{-1}$.
   - The raw derivative $\mu'(t) = \frac{d\mu}{dt} = a b e^{bt}$ is an **absolute** rate of increase with dimensions of $\text{time}^{-2}$. It grows $32\times$ between age 40 and age 80 within a single Gompertz cohort and scales directly with baseline mortality $a$, contradicting `Finch, Pike & Witten (1990)` cited on that same row.
   - **Fix:** Replace `model-free age-specific rise in mortality rate $\mu'(t)$` with the dimensionally correct model-free analogue of $b$—the **age-specific relative rate of hazard rise** (life-table aging rate, $\frac{d}{dt}\ln\mu(t) = \mu'(t)/\mu(t)$)—or simply the **model-free hazard trajectory** $\mu(t)$.

2. **Protocol misstatement on `[^abad2013]` and cross-level citation mismatch on `[^olova2019]` (line 53 and Row 7, line 79):**
   - Line 53 attributes _"continuous whole-body in vivo expression ... killing adult mice through teratomas and disrupted tissue architecture"_ to `[^abad2013]`. In Abad et al. (2013), multi-organ teratomas arose primarily after _transitory_ systemic induction followed by withdrawal, whereas continuous high-dose doxycycline caused acute lethal tissue dysplasia/organ failure within days.
   - Line 53 cites `[^olova2019]` for _"partial reprogramming protocols designed to stop before full loss of somatic identity"_, and Row 7 (`Cellular rejuvenation`) attaches `[^abad2013][^olova2019]` to a contrast between cellular dedifferentiation and organismal functional tissue restoration. Olova et al. (2019) is not an _in vivo_ partial-reprogramming protocol; it is an _in silico_ Horvath epigenetic-clock reanalysis of an _in vitro_ human fibroblast reprogramming time course.
   - **Fix:** Drop the inaccurate `"continuous"` qualifier on `abad2013` (e.g., _"whole-body in vivo expression ... killing adult mice through acute tissue disruption or teratomas"_), and describe `olova2019` accurately as showing an _in vitro_ epigenetic-age decline preceding loss of somatic identity (rather than presenting it as an _in vivo_ partial-reprogramming protocol or organismal restoration proof).
