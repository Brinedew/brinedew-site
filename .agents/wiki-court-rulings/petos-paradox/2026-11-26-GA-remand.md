## Judicial Opinion — `Peto's paradox` (`class: B` → `class: GA` Nomination)

**Ruling:** **Remand to `class: B`** with a bounded refactor order.

`petos-paradox.md` has genuine strengths: calm prose in its best sections, real quantitative instincts, an unusually fair dismantling of the naked mole-rat cell-intrinsic literature, and zero `lint-wiki.mjs` errors. However, it fails multiple objective `GA` gates—including **live rendering failures and local provenance/graph defects that neither external counsel could see**—as well as clear mathematical, forensic, and level-indexing defects established by Alex. Because `content/wiki/` currently contains zero `GA`/`A`/`FA` articles, this ruling also sets the inaugural `GA` precedent for the wiki; waving through broken charts, colliding variables, unindexed _"ageing"_, and one-sided auditing of elephant _TP53_ and bowhead whales would immediately corrupt that bar.

### 2. Rulings on Contested Points Between Alex and Advocate

#### Where Alex's Prosecution Succeeds (Upheld as Remand Requirements)

1. **Mathematical sloppiness in `## The arithmetic` & `### How much extra suppression`:**
   - Reusing $k$ as rate-limiting stages in $C(ut)^k$ and then quoting Nunney's $p = C(ukt)^M$ two sentences later without defining $k$ (division rate) or $M$ (stages) is an unambiguous defect (conceded by Advocate).
   - The prose never bridges age-specific hazard ($\propto t^{k-1}$ in `Terminology`), cumulative expected cancers ($H \propto C(ut)^k$), and lifetime probability ($P(\ge 1) = 1 - e^{-H}$), leaving Figure 1's `0.51` (`-ln(0.60)`) unexplained in body text and leaving US _diagnosis_ risk (~40%) unreconciled with zoo _cancer mortality_ (~5%; humans ~11–25%).
   - Figure 1 mixes Nunney's 2-year mouse lifespan with Cagan's 80%-adult-mortality naked mole-rat lifespan (`25 yr`) on a $t^6$ axis—even though the article's own `somatic-mutation-rates-mammals.csv` lists `Mouse = 3.68 yr`, `Human = 83.7 yr`, and `Naked mole-rat = 25.3 yr` (a ~39× mouse distortion at $k=6$).
   - `### How much extra suppression` leaves a 50,000-fold gap between Caulin (2015)'s `2–3×` and Nunney (2020)'s `>120,000×` unexplained because it never points out the shift from whale-vs-human ($k = 6$) to whale-vs-mouse across a 45× lifespan gap bottlenecked by low-stage ($M = 2$) cancers.
2. **Forensic omissions & inconsistent skepticism across mechanisms:**
   - **Elephant _TP53_:** Must incorporate **Nunney (2022)** (ancestral frameshift prior to duplication; 14 of 19 copies truncated to $\le 88$ aa lacking DNA-binding/oligomerization domains; expansion consistent with neutral duplication/drift, with at most one or a few copies acting via MDM2-decoy/mitochondrial mechanisms), the **germline thermal-protection spandrel** hypothesis (Vollrath 2023; Compton et al. 2024), and **Compton et al. (2025)'s own null cross-species assay** finding no association between fibroblast DNA-damage arrest/apoptosis and neoplasia/malignancy across 15 species. Also disambiguate _"have only one [retrogene]"_.
   - **Bowhead whale (`Firsanov et al. 2025`):** Advocate verified that its Author Correction was clerical, which clears it of image/data misconduct, **but** Alex's double-standard charge stands. The article applies sharp skepticism to the Rochester lab's naked mole-rat claims while putting the same lab's 2025 bowhead fibroblast study (`n = 3` whales in key fibroblast assays, zero wild bowhead cancer epidemiology, _in vitro_ repair + _Drosophila_ lifespan assays) unhedged into the lead alongside 191-species necropsy data, without explaining how CIRBP acts, noting the Arctic-cold-adaptation spandrel alternative, or connecting Firsanov's _"fewer oncogenic hits than human fibroblasts"_ result back to Nunney's stage-count model.
   - **`Rangarajan et al. (2004)`:** Conceded by Advocate. Given its major 2013 Western blot correction across five figures, replace or pair `[^rangarajan2004]` with cleaner transformation evidence and note constitutive telomerase / ultra-long telomeres in laboratory mouse cells.
   - **Naked mole-rat (`Zhang et al. 2023`):** Keep the article's healthy skepticism, but add a concise 2023 update on `nmrHas2` transgenic mice—noting both its modest combined median lifespan gain (+4.4%) awaiting independent replication and its mechanistic irony (anti-inflammatory / non-cell-autonomous effects rather than rescuing cell-intrinsic ECI). Cut the redundant two-sentence summary at the end of line 1136.
3. **False lead premise, missing within-species size tests, and missing core mechanisms:**
   - Lead paragraph 3 falsely asserts that _"researchers identified most in one or two species and have not tested them broadly"_, contradicted by `Gomes et al. (2011)` (>60 mammals), `Cagan et al. (2022)` (16 mammals), and `Compton et al. (2025)` (15 species experimentally assayed).
   - **Within-species size scaling:** Relying solely on Tomasetti & Vogelstein (2015) (`r = 0.81` across tissues, not body sizes, and heavily contested over extrinsic vs. intrinsic risk) while omitting **human height** (~10% higher cancer risk per 10 cm, Nunney 2018) and **domestic dog breeds** (rising with breed size before competing non-cancer mortality shortens giant breeds' lifespan, Nunney 2024) is a genuine conceptual gap.
   - **Tissue architecture & immune policing:** Omitting **hierarchical stem-cell architecture / niche compartmentalization** (`Cairns 1975`; linking `[[Clonal governance]]` and `[[Proliferation-differentiation coupling]]`) and **immunological surveillance** (`Nunney 2020`'s negative quantitative modeling alongside `Vincze et al. 2025`'s comparative MHC class I correlation, linking `[[immune-surveillance|Immune surveillance]]`) leaves out two of the main theoretical and comparative axes of the field.
4. **Shallow conflict mapping in `## The comparative evidence`:**
   - Add Compton et al.'s key **gestation-time** effect (required for malignancy vs. mass to reach significance) and binomial regression ORs, **Dujon et al. (2025)**'s Bayesian reconciliation (mortality vs. prevalence slopes diverging inside wide uncertainty intervals), and **Dujon et al. (2026)**'s captive-species popularity/ascertainment bias finding (which directly challenges small ~2% slopes even though it cannot erase $10^6$-fold naive predictions).
5. **Under-developed, unindexed `## Peto's paradox and ageing`:**
   - Replace bare, unindexed _"ageing"_ with level-indexed quantities (`somatic mutation load`, `replicative arrest`, `tissue frailty`, `actuarial mortality creep` per `Non-technical language in longevity.md`).
   - Fix the mangled summary of **Peto et al. (1975)** (the paper argued cancer incidence tracks _carcinogen exposure duration_, not intrinsic chronological aging—contradicting what line 1150 currently says and already stated more accurately on line 1156!).
   - Briefly state the **Level 2 → Level 3 tumor-suppression trade-off** (e.g., cell elimination via apoptosis/senescence vs. high-fidelity DNA repair) and delegate full mechanics to `[[tumor-suppressor-theory-of-aging|Tumor Suppressor Theory of Aging]]` and `[[Conflict between development and tumor suppression]]`.
6. **Craft, redundancy, self-contradiction & meta-scaffolding (`CLAUDE.md`):**
   - Convert the bold-lead `Author (Year)` bullet catalogs in `## The comparative evidence` and `### Cell size, metabolism and tumour dynamics` into flowing sequential prose with routine author names moved to `[^footnotes]`.
   - Fix the flat **self-contradiction in `## History`** claiming elephant _TP53_ (2015–2016) was _"the first large-species mechanism"_ when `Seluanov (2007)` and `Gomes (2011)` established body-size telomerase repression 4–8 years earlier. Reorder historical/observational grounding toward the front (eliminating the redundant recap at the bottom).
   - Delete the standalone meta-scaffolding `## Data and figures` section (move essential dataset/bandwidth provenance into figure captions or collapsible notes). Fix British/American spelling drift (`centered` → `centred`, `signaling` → `signalling`).
