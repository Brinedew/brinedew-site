# S-Tier Wikipedia Lead Prototypes (Featured Biology Articles)

These are the standard we're aiming for. Every wiki page lead should be tiered against these.

---

## DNA (Featured Article)
> **Deoxyribonucleic acid** is a polymer composed of two polynucleotide chains that coil around each other to form a double helix. The polymer carries genetic instructions for the development, functioning, growth and reproduction of all known organisms and many viruses. DNA and ribonucleic acid (RNA) are nucleic acids. Alongside proteins, lipids and complex carbohydrates (polysaccharides), nucleic acids are one of the four major types of macromolecules that are essential for all known forms of life.

## Cancer
> **Cancer** is a group of diseases involving uncontrolled cell growth typically resulting in tumors with the potential to invade or spread to other parts of the body. These malignant tumors contrast with benign tumors, which do not spread. Over 100 types of cancers affect humans.

## Evolution
> **Evolution** is the change in the heritable characteristics of biological populations over successive generations. It occurs when evolutionary processes such as genetic drift and natural selection act on genetic variation, resulting in certain characteristics becoming more or less common within a population over successive generations. The process of evolution has given rise to biodiversity at every level of biological organisation.

## Gene
> In biology, the word **gene** has two meanings. The Mendelian gene is a basic unit of heredity. The molecular gene is a sequence of nucleotides in DNA that is transcribed to produce RNA. There are two types of molecular genes: protein-coding genes and non-coding genes.

## Telomere
> A **telomere** is a region of repetitive nucleotide sequences associated with specialized proteins at the ends of linear chromosomes. Telomeres are a fundamental genomic security mechanism: they protect the internal regions of the chromosome from degradation and prevent chromosome fusion, while also acting as a timer that limits the number of times a cell can divide.

## Tier Definitions

| Tier | Meaning | Criteria |
|------|---------|----------|
| **S** | Featured-article quality | Bolded title in first sentence, clear definition, established context, explains notability, links to broader concepts, stands alone, ≥3 sentences, neutral tone |
| **A** | Solid lead | Meets most criteria, maybe minor issue (slightly short, missing one contextual link) |
| **B** | Adequate | Has the basics (bolded title + definition) but missing context or too short |
| **C** | Weak | Title present but not bolded, or definition unclear, or no context |
| **D** | Poor | Lead exists but fails multiple criteria |
| **F** | Missing | No lead section at all — just a heading or frontmatter |
