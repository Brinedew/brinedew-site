# E2F transcription factors

**What it is.** Proteins that turn on DNA replication genes; normally restrained by Rb.

**Why it matters here.** Oncogene signals that free E2F push cells into S phase; also trigger senescence if excessive.

**Notes.** Axis: Rb–E2F.
