# Fibrin(ogen)

**What it is.** Blood‑clot mesh protein formed from fibrinogen during coagulation.

**Why it matters here.** Non‑healing wound–like matrices rich in fibrin can support tumor growth and immune shielding.

**Notes.** Context: wound healing, tumor stroma.
