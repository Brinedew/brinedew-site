# Wnt proteins

**What it is.** Family of secreted proteins that regulate stem-cell maintenance and proliferation.

**Why it matters here.** Define tissue zones where division is allowed; misregulation fuels overgrowth.

**Notes.** Pathway: Wnt/β-catenin; Mode: paracrine morphogen.
