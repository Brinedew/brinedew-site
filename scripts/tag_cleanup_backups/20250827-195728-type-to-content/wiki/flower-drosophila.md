# Flower (Drosophila)

**What it is.** Cell-surface protein used in fly “cell competition” to mark less-fit cells.

**Why it matters here.** Illustrates neighborhood selection: winners eliminate losers. Mammalian analogs exist but are less defined.

**Notes.** Type: membrane protein; Concept: fitness tagging.
