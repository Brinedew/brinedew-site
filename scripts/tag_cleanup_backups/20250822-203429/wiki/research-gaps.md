# Research Gaps

## Gap #1 — How long is the **continuous** medusa-phase maintained in *Turritopsis dohrnii* (no reversions)?
**What this gap is:** We lack a documented upper bound (or even a proper survival curve) for *continuous maintenance of the medusa body plan* in *T. dohrnii* when reversions to polyp are disallowed.

**What’s known (relevant facts):**
- *T. dohrnii* medusae reach sexual maturity after **~25–30 days at 20 °C** and **~18–22 days at 22 °C** (16L:8D photoperiod), based on lab culture work that focused on development and ontogeny, not longevity per se. [Martell et al., 2016]
- Medusae of hydrozoans **typically die after releasing gametes**; in *T. dohrnii* they may instead revert to a cyst → polyp, restarting the cycle. Recent experimental work emphasizes that **basic biology is still poorly characterized**, including responses to stress and reversal rates. [Invertebrate Biology, 2024/2025]
- Reversal (medusa → cyst → polyp) is well established from classic through modern studies. [Piraino et al., 1996; Pascual-Torner et al., 2022; Matsumoto et al., 2019/2021]
- For scale-setting across small hydromedusae, **lab medusae lifespans rarely exceed ~3 months** (e.g., *Phialidium* spp.) under culture. This is not *Turritopsis*, but it sets a plausible order-of-magnitude for small hydromedusae kept as medusae. [Roosen-Runge, 1970]

**What’s missing:**  
No peer-reviewed report that tracks **medusa-only** husbandry of *T. dohrnii* into the **multi-month** range with explicit censoring of any reversion events and with a published survival curve (median, variance, tails). The field largely measures time-to-maturity and **reversal** dynamics, not **medusa-phase longevity**.

**Why this matters:**  
If medusae routinely senesce (or die for ecological reasons) within weeks to a few months unless they revert, then the “immortality” is fundamentally a **body-plan switching strategy** rather than unusually durable medusae. Quantifying the medusa-only limit makes that trade-off legible.

**Minimal closure test (publishable protocol):**
- **Design:** Start N ≥ 100 ephyrae; maintain at **20–22 °C**, **16L:8D**, gently aerated kreisel or equivalent; **ad lib Artemia nauplii** with daily water quality checks.  
- **Rule:** **Any** detectable reversal (cyst formation, attached tissue) = **failure event**; exclude from “medusa-continuous” survival.  
- **Tracking:** Daily imaging; weekly morphometrics; Kaplan–Meier survival with right-censoring only for accidental loss unrelated to biology.  
- **Reporting:** Median and 95% CI for medusa-phase longevity; temperature/feeding covariates; raw logs and videos in a repository.

**Decision rule for closing the gap:**  
At least one dataset (N ≥ 100) showing a **survival curve** with **median and 95% CI** for **medusa-only** longevity, under clearly stated conditions and with reversal-censoring.
