# AKT

**What it is.** Central kinase promoting cell survival, metabolism, and growth.

**Why it matters here.** Integrates IGF/insulin and PI3K signals; tilts cells away from apoptosis.

**Notes.** Pathway: PI3K–AKT–mTOR.
