# BCL‑XL (BCL2L1)

**What it is.** Anti‑apoptotic relative of BCL‑2.

**Why it matters here.** Tumor cells often depend on it; complicates therapy because many normal cells need it too.

**Notes.** Mitochondrial survival protein.
