# PD‑L1 (CD274)

**What it is.** Immune “brake” ligand displayed on cells to dampen T‑cell attack.

**Why it matters here.** Tumors upregulate PD‑L1 to evade immune surveillance.

**Notes.** Receptor: PD‑1 on T cells; Therapy: anti‑PD‑1/PD‑L1 antibodies.
