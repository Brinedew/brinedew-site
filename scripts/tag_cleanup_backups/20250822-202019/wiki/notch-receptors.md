# Notch receptors

**What it is.** Contact-dependent signaling receptors that decide cell fates and proliferation restraint.

**Why it matters here.** Enforce local governance between neighbors; can act as tumor suppressors or oncogenes, context-dependent.

**Notes.** Mode: juxtacrine signaling.
