# β-catenin (CTNNB1)

**What it is.** Signal relay for Wnt; also links cadherins to the cytoskeleton.

**Why it matters here.** Stabilized β-catenin drives proliferation and stemness; normally kept low by the destruction complex.

**Notes.** Type: transcriptional co-regulator; Pathway: Wnt/β-catenin.
