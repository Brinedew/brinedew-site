# TAZ (WWTR1)

**What it is.** YAP’s paralog; co-activator modulated by Hippo signaling and tissue stiffness.

**Why it matters here.** Drives growth and EMT-like programs when de-repressed.

**Notes.** Type: co-activator; Pathway: Hippo.
