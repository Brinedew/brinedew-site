# MMPs (Matrix Metalloproteinases)

**What it is.** Enzymes that cut extracellular matrix proteins.

**Why it matters here.** SASP‑associated MMPs loosen tissue structure, aiding both normal repair and tumor spread.

**Notes.** Require zinc; inhibited by TIMPs.
