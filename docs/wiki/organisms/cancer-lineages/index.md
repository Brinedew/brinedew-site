# Cancer Lineages

Immortal cancer cell lines that have evolved as independent organisms.

## Overview

Cancer lineages represent a unique form of evolution where cancer cells have become transmissible between hosts, effectively becoming new species.

## Examples

- **[CTVT](ctvt.md)** - Canine Transmissible Venereal Tumor
- **[DFTD](dftd.md)** - Devil Facial Tumor Disease
- **[HeLa](hela.md)** - Henrietta Lacks immortal cell line
