# Longevity Research Wiki

A hierarchical knowledge base for aging biology, longevity research, and cellular mechanisms.

## Core Concepts

Theoretical frameworks for understanding aging:

- **[De-Darwinization](concepts/de-darwinization.md)** - How evolution suppresses evolution within organisms
- **[Death Pact](concepts/death-pact.md)** - The multicellular contract limiting cellular autonomy
- **[Replicative Credit](concepts/replicative-credit.md)** - Investment in high-fidelity DNA replication
- **[SCANDAL Hypothesis](concepts/scandal-hypothesis.md)** - Why sea creatures die when cells could survive
- **[Principal-Agent Problem](concepts/principal-agent-problem.md)** - Managing 37 trillion cellular agents

## Theories

Evolutionary frameworks for understanding aging:

- **[Selection Shadow Theory](theories/selection-shadow-theory.md)** - Why natural selection tolerates aging
- **[Antagonistic Pleiotropy Theory](theories/antagonistic-pleiotropy-theory.md)** - Genes beneficial early, harmful later
- **[Disposable Soma Theory](theories/disposable-soma-theory.md)** - Resource allocation trade-offs
- **[Defensive Degeneration Theory](theories/defensive-degeneration-theory.md)** - Aging as cancer prevention
