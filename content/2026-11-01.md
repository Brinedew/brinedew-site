- Why does **miR-145** exist? It acts as a **Schmitt trigger** (a bistable circuit element in electronics). OCT4 represses miR-145, and miR-145 degrades OCT4. When OCT4 drops below a critical threshold, miR-145 spikes, rapidly chewing up any leftover mRNA and preventing the cell from wavering back.
