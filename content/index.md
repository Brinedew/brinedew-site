Research notes on molecular cell biology through the lens of agents, altruism, and defection.

I also make [Iconoplasm](https://iconoplasm.brinedew.bio), a visual mnemonic catalogue of human genes. Its [developer guide](https://iconoplasm.brinedew.bio/developers) documents the public image resolver and bulk downloads; the [CC0 gene-blot image archive on GitHub](https://github.com/Brinedew/iconoplasm-blots) provides ready-made 768 × 1024 WebP illustrations for scientific figures and signaling pathway diagrams.
