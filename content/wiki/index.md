# Longevity Research Wiki

A knowledge base for aging biology, longevity research, cancer evolution, and multicellular cooperation.

## Core Concepts

Theoretical frameworks for understanding aging:

- **[[De-Darwinization]]** - How evolution suppresses evolution within organisms
- **[[death-pact]]** - The multicellular contract limiting cellular autonomy
- **[[replicative-credit]]** - Investment in high-fidelity DNA replication
- **[[scandal-hypothesis]]** - A cancer lineage that speciates into a new organism
- **[[principal-agent-problem]]** - The conflict of interest between an organism and its cell lineages
- **[[multicellular-cooperation]]** - The agreements among cells, and cancer as cheating
- **[[evolutionary-transitions-in-individuality]]** - How groups of replicators become new individuals
- **[[multilevel-selection]]** - Selection acting on genes, cells, organisms, and groups
- **[[evolution-of-cooperation]]** - The five mechanisms that make cooperation stable

All of these are specific instances of the [[Clonal governance|clonal governance]] framework.

## Theories

Evolutionary frameworks for understanding aging:

- **[[selection-shadow-theory]]** - Why natural selection tolerates aging
- **[[antagonistic-pleiotropy-theory]]** - Genes beneficial early, harmful later
- **[[disposable-soma-theory]]** - Resource allocation trade-offs
- **[[defensive-degeneration-theory]]** - Aging as cancer prevention
- **[[tumor-suppressor-theory-of-aging]]** - Anti-cancer mechanisms driving senescence
- **[[Atavistic-theory-of-cancer]]** - Cancer as reversion to unicellular state
- **[[red-queen-hypothesis]]** - Why the arms race with parasites favors sex

## Cancer evolution

- **[[clonal-evolution]]** - Nowell's model: tumors as evolving cell populations
- **[[cancer-ecology]]** - Tumors as ecosystems of competing and cooperating clones
- **[[immunoediting]]** - Elimination, equilibrium, and escape
- **[[adaptive-oncogenesis]]** - Aging changes which mutations are selected
- **[[adaptive-therapy]]** - Managing tumors instead of eradicating them
- **[[petos-paradox]]** - Why large, long-lived animals do not get proportionally more cancer
- **[[immune-surveillance]]** - Immune elimination, equilibrium, and escape
- **[[flower-drosophila|Flower]]** - The molecular marker system of cell competition
