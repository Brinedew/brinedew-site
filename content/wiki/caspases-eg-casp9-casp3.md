# Caspases (e.g., CASP9, CASP3)

**What it is.** Proteases that dismantle the cell during apoptosis.

**Why it matters here.** Final executioners in the kill program; blocking them prevents clean cell death.

**Notes.** Intrinsic: caspase‑9; Executioner: caspase‑3/7.
