# HLA-A (HLA-A)

**What it is.** Cell-surface receptor that presents peptide fragments to CD8 T cells.

**Why it matters here.** Loss hides cells from T cells but can invite NK cell attack; a common tumor evasion route.

**Notes.** Concept: antigen presentation.
