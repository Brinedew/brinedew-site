# p16^INK4a (CDKN2A)

**What it is.** Cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor that locks Rb in its active state and prevents cell-cycle entry.

**Why it matters here.** Core effector of oncogene-induced senescence; often deleted in cancers.

**Notes.** Type: CKI; Axis: p16–CDK4/6–Rb–E2F.
