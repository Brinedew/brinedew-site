# p53 (TP53)

**What it is.** Transcription factor that integrates many cellular stress signals and decides between temporary arrest, DNA repair, senescence, or apoptosis.

**Why it matters here.** Central decision hub for cell-intrinsic policing; loss-of-function is among the most common changes in human cancers.

**Notes.** Type: tumor suppressor; Pathways: DNA damage response (ATM/ATR→p53), ARF–MDM2–p53.
