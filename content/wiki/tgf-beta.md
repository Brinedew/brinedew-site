# TGF‑β

**What it is.** Multifunctional cytokine that can suppress early tumors yet promote late-stage invasion and immune evasion.

**Why it matters here.** Part of the wound-healing program that tumors appropriate to grow and hide.

**Notes.** Pathway: SMADs.
