# Fibrinogen alpha chain (FGA)

**What it is.** Component of fibrinogen; upon thrombin cleavage, contributes to fibrin mesh during coagulation.

**Why it matters here.** Non-healing wound-like matrices rich in fibrin can support tumor growth and immune shielding.

**Notes.** Complex: fibrinogen (FGA/FGB/FGG).
