# QSOX1

**What it is.** Catalyzes the oxidation of sulfhydryl groups in peptide and protein thiols to disulfides with the reduction of oxygen to hydrogen peroxide (PubMed:17331072, PubMed:18393449, PubMed:23704371, PubMed:23867277, PubMed:30367560).
