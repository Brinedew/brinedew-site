# MDM2

**What it is.** E3 ubiquitin ligase that targets p53 for degradation.

**Why it matters here.** Amplification reduces p53 levels and weakens intrinsic policing.

**Notes.** Type: ubiquitin ligase; Regulator: inhibited by ARF.
