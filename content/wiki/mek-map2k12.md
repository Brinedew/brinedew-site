# MEK (MAP2K1/2)

**What it is.** Kinases activated by RAF to phosphorylate ERK.

**Why it matters here.** Bottleneck in the core mitogen pathway; drug target in multiple cancers.

**Notes.** Pathway: RAS–RAF–MEK–ERK.
