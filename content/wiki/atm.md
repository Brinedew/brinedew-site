# ATM

**What it is.** DNA damage sensor kinase that responds mainly to double-strand breaks and activates p53 and repair programs.

**Why it matters here.** Starts the arrest/senescence/apoptosis cascade when DNA is damaged.

**Notes.** Type: serine/threonine kinase; Partners: ATR, p53.
