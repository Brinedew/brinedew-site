# Telomerase (TERT)

**What it is.** Reverse transcriptase that extends telomeres at chromosome ends.

**Why it matters here.** Reactivation lets cells bypass the normal “replicative retirement” limit and divide indefinitely.

**Notes.** Type: polymerase; Also see: ALT (telomere maintenance mechanism).
