# MMP9 (MMP9)

**What it is.** Metalloprotease that cuts extracellular matrix proteins.

**Why it matters here.** SASP‑associated MMPs loosen tissue structure, aiding both normal repair and tumor spread.

**Notes.** Requires zinc; inhibited by TIMPs.
