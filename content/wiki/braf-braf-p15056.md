# BRAF (BRAF)

**What it is.** Kinase that relays RAS signals to MEK/ERK.

**Why it matters here.** Mutant BRAF (e.g., V600E) gives strong growth drive; targeted by specific inhibitors.

**Notes.** Pathway: MAPK cascade.
