# VEGF (VEGFA)

**What it is.** Vascular growth factor that stimulates new blood vessel formation.

**Why it matters here.** Tumors secrete VEGF to secure blood supply (angiogenesis).

**Notes.** Receptors: VEGFR1/2; Therapy: anti‑angiogenics.
