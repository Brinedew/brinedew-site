# BCL‑2

**What it is.** Mitochondrial membrane protein that blocks apoptosis.

**Why it matters here.** Overexpression makes cells resistant to death; targeted by drugs (e.g., venetoclax).

**Notes.** Family: anti‑apoptotic BCL‑2 proteins.
