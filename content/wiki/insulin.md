# Insulin

**What it is.** Pancreatic hormone controlling glucose uptake and anabolic metabolism.

**Why it matters here.** Indirectly shifts proliferation propensity via PI3K–AKT–mTOR; high insulin can favor growth signals.

**Notes.** Receptor: INSR.
