# E2F1 (E2F1)

**What it is.** Transcription factor that turns on DNA replication genes; normally restrained by RB.

**Why it matters here.** Oncogenic signals that free E2F1 push cells into S phase; excessive drive can trigger senescence or apoptosis.

**Notes.** Axis: RB–E2F.
