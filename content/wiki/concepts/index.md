# Core Concepts

Theoretical frameworks for understanding aging and longevity.

## Overview

This section covers the fundamental conceptual frameworks that help us understand how aging works at a systems level.

## Concepts

- **[De-Darwinization](de-darwinization.md)** - How evolution suppresses evolution within organisms
- **[Death Pact](death-pact.md)** - The multicellular contract limiting cellular autonomy
- **[Replicative Credit](replicative-credit.md)** - Investment in high-fidelity DNA replication
- **[SCANDAL Hypothesis](scandal-hypothesis.md)** - Why sea creatures die when cells could survive
- **[Principal-Agent Problem](principal-agent-problem.md)** - Managing 37 trillion cellular agents
