# EGF (EGF)

**What it is.** Mitogen that binds EGFR to stimulate cell division.

**Why it matters here.** Example of a local "permit" signal; autocrine EGF loops can make cells self-sufficient.

**Notes.** Receptor: EGFR (ERBB1).
