# Sonic hedgehog (SHH)

**What it is.** Secreted morphogen that controls growth and differentiation during development and in adult niches.

**Why it matters here.** Abnormal activation can sustain proliferation outside proper zones.

**Notes.** Pathway: Hedgehog→PTCH→SMO→GLI.
