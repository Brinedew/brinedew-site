# Tier List: Cell lines

Here is the **Cell Line Tier List**.

---

### **S Tier: The Pantheon (Every Biologist Must Know)**
*These are foundational to modern medicine, biopharma, and cell biology. You cannot pass a molecular biology qualifying exam without knowing them.*

* **HeLa**: The progenitor of them all. Henrietta Lacks’ immortal cervical carcinoma; ubiquitous, indestructible, and responsible for countless Nobel prizes.
* **HEK 293 / 293T**: The viral packaging and protein expression kingpin of biotechnology and synthetic biology.
* **CHO**: Chinese Hamster Ovary cells. The billion-dollar biopharma engine that churns out the majority of therapeutic monoclonal antibodies.
* **Jurkat**: **The undisputed sovereign of T-cell biology.** Isolated from a teenage leukemia patient in the 1970s, almost everything we know about T-cell receptor (TCR) signaling, T-cell activation, and early apoptosis biochemistry was mapped out in Jurkat suspension cultures.
* **MCF-7**: **The queen of oncology.** Derived from the pleural effusion of Sister Catherine Frances Mallon in 1970. It is the gold standard ER+/PR+ luminal breast cancer line. If a paper discusses estrogen signaling, endocrine therapy resistance, or SERMs (like tamoxifen), MCF-7 is guaranteed to be in Figure 1.

---

### **A Tier: The Heavyweights (Found in Nearly Every Cancer Paper)**
*Ubiquitous across oncology, high-throughput drug screens, and mechanistic studies. Most life scientists encounter these regularly.*

* **MDA-MB-231**: The undisputed king of Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC) models. Highly invasive, mesenchymal, spindle-shaped, and the default choice for metastasis and transwell invasion assays.
* **A-549**: The absolute gold standard for Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC, KRAS-mutant) and respiratory infection models (influenza, adenoviruses, SARS-CoV-2). Grows rapidly and reliably.
* **LNCaP & PC-3**: The classic prostate cancer duo. 
  * **LNCaP**: Androgen-dependent, PSA-secreting, AR-positive workhorse.
  * **PC-3**: The high-grade, AR-negative, bone-metastatic, castration-resistant bruiser.
* **PANC-1**: The most cited pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) cell line. Harboring classic *KRAS* and *TP53* mutations, it is the primary workhorse for pancreatic cancer chemotherapy resistance.
* **MCF-10A**: The standard non-tumorigenic "normal" control line for mammary epithelium. If someone makes an oncogenic claim in breast cancer, reviewers will demand, *"Did you show this doesn't happen in MCF-10A?"*
* **U2OS / HepG2 / K562** *(External benchmarks)*: The universal standards for cell biology microscopy (U2OS), hepatotoxicity/liver screens (HepG2), and CML/leukemia suspension studies (K562).

---

### **B Tier: Specialized Champions (Essential Subtype Models)**
*Standard-bearers within their specific niches. In their respective subfields, they are essential.*

* **SK-BR-3**: The premier **HER2-amplified** breast cancer line. Trastuzumab (Herceptin) and ADC assays rely on this as their primary model.
* **A-375**: The poster child for malignant **melanoma** driven by the famous **BRAF V600E** mutation. Essential for studying MAPK/ERK signaling and RAF/MEK inhibitors.
* **786-O**: The default clear cell **renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC)** model. It carries a homozygous *VHL* mutation, making it the classic line for studying HIF-1α/2α deregulation and pseudo-hypoxia.
* **SK-OV-3**: The most widely published **ovarian adenocarcinoma** cell line. (Though debated whether it truly reflects high-grade serous histology, it remains an indispensable screening tool).
* **DU145**: The "middle child" of prostate cancer. Moderately metastatic, androgen-insensitive, brain metastasis-derived. Rounds out the tri-panel alongside PC-3 and LNCaP.
* **MDA-MB-468**: Essential basal-like TNBC line. Famous for carrying heavy **EGFR amplification** and a *PTEN* deletion, contrasting with MDA-MB-231.

---

### **C Tier: The Panel Fillers & Second-String Options**
*Respected, well-characterized, but typically drafted only when Reviewer 3 asks for "a second or third cell line to confirm findings."*

* **BxPC-3**: High utility in pancreatic cancer because it is **KRAS wild-type**—an anomaly in PDAC used specifically as a negative control against KRAS-mutant lines like PANC-1.
* **HCC1937**: Crucial for **germline BRCA1-mutant** breast cancer research and PARP-inhibitor resistance studies, but finicky to culture and strictly niche.
* **MG-63**: A reliable osteosarcoma line, but lives perpetually in the shadow of **U2OS** and **Saos-2**.
* **BT-549**: A secondary mesenchymal TNBC cell line. Almost never the headline model; primarily used to back up findings from MDA-MB-231.
* **A-172**: A viable glioblastoma line, but largely overshadowed by the dominant U-87 / U-251 glioblastoma lineage.

---

### **D Tier: The Niche / Redundant Benchwarmers**
*Lines that are rarely picked as primary models today due to changing technologies or better alternatives.*

* **T98G**: A glioblastoma line famous historically for resting in a reversible G1 arrest state under low serum. It has high MGMT expression (conferring temozolomide resistance), but outside of niche cell cycle or MGMT DNA-repair papers, modern neuro-oncology labs rarely default to it.

---

### **F Tier: The Disgraced, Cross-Contaminated, and Obsolete**
*The cautionary tales of cell culture history. Papers relying on these without addressing their flaws are immediate red flags.*

* **MDA-MB-435**: *The Great Impostor.* Used for nearly **three decades** as a flagship metastatic breast cancer line in hundreds of funded studies. Microarrays and STR profiling eventually proved it was an **M14 melanoma** cross-contamination.
* **HEp-2 & KB**: Long published as "laryngeal carcinoma" and "oral epidermoid carcinoma"—both were exposed decades ago as aggressive **HeLa overgrowth**.
* **Chang Liver**: Originally marketed as "normal human liver cells"—turned out to be **HeLa** yet again.
* **ATCC U-87 MG (The Identity Crisis)**: A cautionary tale in neuro-oncology. In 2016, DNA profiling proved the widely distributed ATCC stock of U-87 MG did **not** match the original glioblastoma patient tumor established at Uppsala in 1968 due to a historical cross-contamination event (though labs still use it out of sheer inertia).
