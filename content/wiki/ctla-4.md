# CTLA-4 (CTLA4)

**What it is.** T-cell inhibitory receptor that competes with CD28 for co-stimulatory ligands (CD80/CD86).

**Why it matters here.** Key immune brake exploited by tumors; validated therapeutic target (checkpoint blockade).

**Notes.** Therapy: anti-CTLA-4 antibodies (e.g., ipilimumab).
