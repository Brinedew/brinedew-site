**What it is.** Inflammatory cytokine that promotes acute‑phase responses and can support growth.

**Why it matters here.** Prominent in the senescence‑associated secretory phenotype (SASP), reshaping tissue context.

**Notes.** Source: many cell types, including senescent cells.
