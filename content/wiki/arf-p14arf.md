# ARF (p14^ARF)

**What it is.** Tumor suppressor produced from the CDKN2A locus; stabilizes p53 by inhibiting MDM2.

**Why it matters here.** Links hyperactive growth signals to p53 activation; often silenced in tumors.

**Notes.** Type: p53 regulator; Axis: ARF–MDM2–p53.
