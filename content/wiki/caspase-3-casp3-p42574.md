# Caspase-3 (CASP3)
**What it is.** Executioner cysteine protease that dismantles cells during apoptosis by cleaving key structural and regulatory proteins.

**Why it matters here.** Central executioner in apoptosis; its activation commits the cell to orderly death, while inhibition blocks clean dismantling.

**Notes.** Executioner caspase; activated downstream of initiators (e.g., CASP9). Representative of the caspase family for this wiki entry.
