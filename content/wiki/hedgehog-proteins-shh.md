# Hedgehog proteins (Shh)

**What it is.** Secreted patterning signals that control growth and differentiation during development and in adult niches.

**Why it matters here.** Abnormal activation can sustain proliferation outside proper zones.

**Notes.** Pathway: Hedgehog–Gli.
