# AKT1

**What it is.** Serine/threonine kinase that regulates cell survival, growth, and metabolism downstream of PI3K.

**Why it matters here.** Central node in growth signaling; hyperactivation promotes cancer, while dysfunction affects metabolic aging.

**Notes.** Type: protein kinase; Pathways: PI3K/AKT/mTOR, insulin signaling.
