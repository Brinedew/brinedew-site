# BAK (BAK1)

**What it is.** Pro-apoptotic partner of BAX; forms pores in mitochondria to trigger cell suicide.

**Why it matters here.** Loss impairs apoptosis; tumors often tilt the balance toward survival.

**Notes.** Type: BCL-2 family effector.
