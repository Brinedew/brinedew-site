# Mitosis vetoes

Separately from "when to initiate division", cell has "when to block division" genes.

Tumor suppressors likely evolved from these.

Here I list conceptual categories of mitosis vetoes. 

In prokaryotes.

E.coli

Min proteins suppress polar Z-rings, 

nucleoid occlusion suppresses septation over DNA,

division is coordinated with chromosome replication and segregation.


_M. genitalium_ looks much more stripped down than that.

In mycoplasmas, Martínez-Torró et al. write: “In mycoplasmas, the dcw gene cluster is usually limited to four genes: mraZ, mraW, MG_223, and ftsZ.” in _M. genitalium_ they found: “the entire cell division gene cluster of _M. genitalium_ is non-essential for growth in vitro.”
