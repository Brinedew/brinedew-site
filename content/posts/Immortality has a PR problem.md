# Immortality has a PR problem

1. Achieving immortality makes is not a compelling narrative to most
	1. Rich get richer problem
	2. Villains act, heroes react
	3. The most moving motivator is Self-sacrifice 
2. What narrative works:
	1. Expedition 33 plot
	2. Parallels from expedition 33 to aging research
	3. What would make for a movement that people root to succeed

As aging researchers, we're walking on bones of those who came before us.
So let our gravestones be landmarks.
