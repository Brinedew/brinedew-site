# Somatic Engineering Manifesto
### Design principles for clonal multi-agent systems



    
5. Sacrifice many: Most cells are cannon fodder. The math is stark: 37 trillion cells, maybe a few thousand matter for lineage continuation. The rest are tissue paper.
    
6. Loyalty over competence: This is counterintuitive. You'd think evolution would pick the best cells. But the "best" are the most dangerous. So it picks the most docile. Efficiency is a liability.
    
7. Nail hammered: Immune surveillance and cell competition are relentless censors. They don't care about potential benefits of variation. Variation = risk.
    
8. Specialize by forgetting: You literally break the genome's ability to access other states. It's like welding doors shut. Makes tissues brittle but safe.
    
9. Suicide: Apoptosis thresholds are set to kill false positives. Better to lose good cells than keep bad ones. It's a draconian policy.
    
10. Empty husks: These are brilliant. You strip cells of everything that makes them alive so they can't rebel. It's like building your house from bricks that used to be people.
    
11. Aging extends lifespan: This is the cruelest one. The same thing that protects you (senescence) slowly kills you. But the alternative is faster death by cancer.


12. Defection is an existential risk
	1. Every cell is a potential cancer. Takes only a few transgressions to bring down everyone - sometimes less.
13. Abandon your friends
	1. Single-cell bottlenecks enforce uniformity and trust - at least initially. All that preceding somatic cooperation? Deleted. It feels wasteful but starting alone is the best way to delay runaway selection within the body.
14. Burn the boats
	1. Cooperation is stronger when there's no escape route. When a fish dies, its cells could float outwards into the open sea and survive. They don't. They choose to die with the fish and honor the death pact. 
15. From immortal make mortal
	1. Mortal cells can only replicate so many times before running out of life.
16. Sacrifice many to serve the few
	1. Disposable soma serves the immortal germline
