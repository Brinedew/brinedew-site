# Somatic Engineering Manifesto
## Design principles for clonal multi-agent systems

1. Betrayal is an existential risk
	1. A single unstoppable replicator can bring down the entire system
2. Leave your friends behind
	1. Single-cell bottlenecks enforce uniformity and trust - at least initially
3. Burn the boats
	1. When a fish dies, its cells could float outwards into the sea and survive. They don't. They choose to die with the fish.
4. From immortal make mortal
	1. The less immortal cells there are, the less
5. Sacrifice many to serve the few
6. Loyalty over competence
7. The nail that sticks out gets hammered down
8. Specialize by forgetting
9. Suicide under stress
10. Empty husks can't betray you
11. Aging extends lifespan
