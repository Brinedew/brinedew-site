# Iconoplasm - Caretaker's Handbook

## Who are caretakers?

Any Iconoplasm user can visit a gene page and become that gene's **caretaker**.

**It's a 1-to-1 match:** each gene has only one caretaker, and a caretaker can only care for one gene at a time.

However, **it's not a commitment:** as a caretaker, you can switch genes anytime.

Caretakers have great control over the gene's identity as a character:

- Caretakers can modify the lore snippet of the gene (up to 10,000 characters long).
- Caretakers can modify the gene's visual tags that are used for image generation.
- Caretakers can give their one transferable supervote to the candidate portrait that they like best.

All of these changes instantly propagate to the engine that generates images. As a result, here's what you can do in just 10 minutes:
1. See a new gene that you like
2. Become its caretaker 
3. Modify the gene's visual tags to your liking (see [[apps/iconoplasm/docs/generation-tips|Generation tips]])
4. Order new free portraits in your favorite styles
5. See new portraits arrive 
6. And finally, give your supervote to the best portrait so it becomes a canonical blot for that gene.

Caretakers can help the Iconoplasm project in multiple ways:

- **Worldbuilding:** you can modify the gene's lore and appearance in a way that suits your creative narrative. See [[apps/iconoplasm/docs/write-your-genes-story|Write your gene's story]].
- **Quality control:** if the gene's portraits consistently differ from the value that is assigned to the gene on its card, you can become a caretaker and correct the deviation.
- **Social coordination:** groups of caretakers can coordinate to propose a better mapping for a clan or a gene family.

## Who can become a caretaker?

At the moment, anyone with a Discord account can become a caretaker. You don't need to be a member of the Brinedew.bio Discord to be a caretaker. This might change in the future.

## Do I need to have published research on this gene to become its caretaker?

No, interest alone is enough. My hope is that biology students and newcomers to the field will first learn the biology of their chosen gene. Then they will learn foundational biological concepts through that character, and finally use their gene as a starting point for exploring other parts of molecular cell biology.

To get up to speed after becoming a caretaker, try to get familiar with the basics:

- Your gene's protein products, their subcellular location and molecular function.
- Your gene's downstream effects in normal and pathological states.
- Your gene's expression pattern across tissues and developmental stages.
- What other genes are similar to yours in structure and function.
- What other genes interact with yours both at the protein level and at the level of transcriptional regulation.

## How long can I stay a caretaker of a single gene?

There is no time limit: you can settle on your favorite gene and stay its caretaker for as long as you'd like. A vision for Iconoplasm is that each gene will eventually have had the same caretaker for decades.

However, it's possible that you could be removed from the caretaker position against your will. One example would be going silent for months at a time despite getting coordination requests from other caretakers. Other such removal policies might be implemented in the future to keep the project thriving.

## How many times can I change which gene I care for?

While you can only care for one gene at a time, there's no limit on how many times you can change which gene you're a caretaker for.

You can currently change genes once every 15 minutes. In the future, I will make this limit stricter. That way, the assignment of caretakers to genes may start out a bit chaotic but will gradually become stable.

## What if I want to swap genes with another caretaker?

Go to a gene page and click the "Request swap" button in the gene toolbar. The caretaker for that gene will get a notification of the swap request and can either accept or reject it. You can only have one swap request active at a time.

## What happens when two caretakers can't agree on a shared story?

For now, I will adjudicate the conflicts personally. In the future, a formal system for resolving conflicts might get implemented.

## Can the displayed lore snippet on a gene page conflict with the canonical image on the same page?

Yes, but not forever. 

One case where this might happen is if you're trying to redesign a popular character. You can make a fresh portrait for the new design pretty much instantly, but if the old canonical portrait was highly upvoted, it will take some time for the new candidate to get enough votes to replace it.
## If I contribute work, how will it be used?

I expect that other people might use the character designs in their own content:

- Adding character images to pathway diagrams
- Making mnemonic flashcard decks
- Featuring the characters in books, videos, and games
- Selling merch or figurines based on the character

**Please note:** as explained in the [caretaker terms](https://iconoplasm.brinedew.bio/caretaker-terms), when you contribute to Iconoplasm, you dedicate your creative work (lore writing and curation) to the public domain under the CC0 license where applicable. This means that anyone else can modify your Iconoplasm design, monetize it, and distribute it as they see fit, without needing to credit you.
