# Remove an artist's style


You can use the [blocklist form](https://iconoplasm.brinedew.bio/blocklist) to remove your style from the gallery.

Enter the exact @name tag as it appears in the library of artist tags: <https://thetacursed.github.io/Anima-Style-Explorer/>. You need to log in to the brinedew.bio website with your Discord account to get past the bot protection on the blocklist form. 

If you don't want to log in to the site with Discord, you can send your blocklist request by email to <blocklist@brinedew.bio> from your business's email account. The first @name that you send me will be added to the blocklist. Make sure to use the @ symbol in front of the name for the email to be reviewed automatically.
