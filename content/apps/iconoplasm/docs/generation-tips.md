# Generation tips


## I struggle to show a character's weight in the image

By default, the auto-tagger adds descriptive tags like "petite" or "sturdy" to the character's tag cloud. However, viewers may not get an intuitive sense of the character's mass from these tags. The problem becomes bigger at the extremes, like 10 kg or 500 kg characters.

In my testing, I found three approaches that let viewers tell a character's weight from the image:

1. Specify a head-to-body ratio as a separate character tag.

https://pinterest.com/pin/476326098091768543/

Some rough numbers that you can take as a starting point:

| Weight | How many heads tall |
| ------ | ------------------- |
| 2 kg   | 2                   |
| 5 kg   | 3                   |
| 10 kg  | 4                   |
| 20 kg  | 5                   |
| 40 kg  | 6                   |
| 60 kg  | 7                   |
| 80 kg  | 8                   |
| 120 kg | 9                   |
| 160 kg | 10                  |

2. "Banana for scale" method: add action tags that describe how big or small the surrounding props are in relation to your character. Viewers read a character who is "dwarfed by the height of the doorframe" as smaller in mass than a character who "crouches to fit into doorframe".
3. Name a known character archetype: "hulk physique", "goblin proportions", "chibi".
