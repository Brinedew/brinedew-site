<h1 class="sr-only">Iconoplasm - gene character cards and browser extension</h1>

<!-- ARCHITECTURE FENCE [IPD-003]: this existing non-visual description owns the crawler frontier. Do not add /genes as visible application chrome. -->
<p class="sr-only">Iconoplasm is the gene humanization database: it turns 19,023 human gene symbols into memorable character cards called blots. Here gene humanization means visual personification, not replacing animal genes with human orthologs. Search by gene symbol or name, browse <a href="https://iconoplasm.brinedew.bio/genes" tabindex="-1">published human gene cards</a>, retrieve labelled character images through the public image resolver, and use the browser extension to recognize genes while reading papers, databases, and other biology pages.</p>
