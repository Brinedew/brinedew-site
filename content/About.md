## About
Hi. I'm a wet lab biologist, and I made this site to host my notes and interactive projects.

The writing here falls into two categories:

1. **[Posts](https://brinedew.bio/tags/content/post)** are written by me, in a shorter format 

2. **[Wiki](https://brinedew.bio/tags/content/wiki)** pages are heavily AI-assisted. I use them for non-controversial primers and memory aid.

I'm a researcher, not a medical doctor. The content of this site is not medical advice, and I don't endorse any health interventions.
